An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty "One Word From Grandma": exactly one word — the word a grandma would text about AI.

## The word

> Doohickey

**9 characters** (gate: 1–30). One word, no spaces, no punctuation.

## Concept

Grandma does not explain the machine. She names it the way she names every
mysterious box with blinking lights in her house: a doohickey. It is the
word grandmothers keep in their pocket for any gadget they cannot quite
describe, and AI is the ultimate one — clever, humming, and impossible to
explain at the dinner table. Warm, a little baffled, and funny on its own,
with no caption needed.

## How it works

`main.py` holds the word as a constant, checks it against the brief's gate
(1–30 characters, exactly one word, letters only, no whitespace or control
characters), prints it, and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`. Python
standard library only; the output is fully deterministic.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
